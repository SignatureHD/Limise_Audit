Three Urgent Issues

1. Remove Prototype Content

Live on your site: "This is where the booking calendar goes..." and "A live chat widget will sit here..." These are internal build notes visible to prospects.

2. Replace Zero Metrics

"Real Impact. Real Results" displays 0%, 0, 0 and 0. Visually reads as "Limise achieved zero." Remove until real data exists.

3. Reconcile Your Story

Website and company profile disagree. Six services described, five shown. Leadership titles inconsistent. Solve as one brand exercise.

The real sales story—connecting compliance, learning, strategy and measurement—is buried. Make it your homepage hero.
